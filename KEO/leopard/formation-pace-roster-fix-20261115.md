# Formation pace on an unavailable roster (private phase 2, 2026-11-15)

The formation poll refused a missing player singleton, missing roster, empty roster, or a roster above200 characters before clearing/publishing gather pace. The native getSpeed wrapper still consumed the previously published factors. A former gathering member could therefore keep its old speed reduction indefinitely while the poll could not validate its membership.

PollFormationGroups now starts an empty pace stage before roster validation and publishes it on both refusal paths. Group/member state is preserved for a later valid roster; no character is inspected or moved on those paths. The valid poll retains one Begin, its usual group/follow work and one Publish. Unarmed pace remains a no-op. No new scan, allocation, native call, timer or counter was added. The separate dependency on camera/preload/idle gates is unchanged and still under review.
