# Planner replan fairness (private phase 2, 2026-11-15)

The existing 250ms planner tick always visited slots0..63 and allowed four total replan attempts. An attempt counted even when the goal/start could not be located. Such a failed attempt leaves its due trigger and plan timestamps untouched. Four permanently failing plans in slots0..3 could therefore monopolize every later tick, indefinitely preventing a due plan in slot4 or later from getting a turn.

The one-pass slot traversal now starts after the most recent attempted replan. Every one of the64 slot keys is still checked once, every active slot is still validated once, and at most four attempts occur per tick. TickSlot and the meaning of an attempt/success/counter are unchanged. Empty slots consume no budget. No extra slot scan, allocation, timer, native call or engine scheduling change was added. The cursor is main-thread-only; world generation reset returns it to0; OFF, pause, over-cap player lists and ticks rejected by the250ms cadence leave it untouched. The coarse-graph retention question is separate and unchanged.

Fail-first evidence: `build/planner-replan-fairness/red-original-run.txt` records the actual pre-fix production PlannerTick failing `attempts[4]==1` after two ticks with four persistent low-slot failures. The fixture extracts the actual production outer tick and slot traversal; only engine/slot dependencies are substituted, including persistent failed-attempt effects. It does not mirror the scheduler in a pure helper.

Validation:216 actual-production scheduling assertions pass in VC10 /Od and /O2: failed head slots/later success, all64 key/active validations, attempt bounds, capacity1/7/63/64 wrap, empty slot63, zero budget, cadence, OFF/pause/overcap, ordinary and paused generation reset. Eight compiled production mutants fail the expected runtime assertions: fixed start, no cursor advance, budget-sized cursor jump, hardcoded wrap, unbounded attempts, validation cut at budget, overcap traversal and missing world reset. The actual changed production TU compiles separately in DEV and PROD. Logs are in `build/planner-replan-fairness`.

No game, process access, profiling, capture, full DLL build, installation or source-checkout edit was performed. This fixes a provable fairness defect; it makes no measured FPS claim. Native TickSlot body is unchanged; the scheduler fixture substitutes its effects and does not execute actual route searches or engine calls. Independent review is required before integration.
